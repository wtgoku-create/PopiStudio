# Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering POPi MCN avatarized media.

## Identity

- The intended person or pet is replaced.
- The character matches the provided reference image or text description.
- The same character remains stable across image batches or video duration.
- Unrelated people, pets, props, and background details remain unchanged unless requested.

## Image

- Original pose, framing, lighting, and scene logic are preserved.
- Subject edges blend with the background.
- Hands, paws, eyes, ears, hair, tails, accessories, and clothing are plausible for the chosen style.
- The output is not an unintended celebrity or real-person lookalike.

## Video

- The subject follows the original action and camera rhythm.
- The character does not drift between shots or over time.
- There is no obvious flicker, face collapse, limb breakage, pet deformation, or background melting.
- The output keeps the original shareable vlog feeling instead of becoming an unrelated cinematic scene.
- Audio is preserved or intentionally regenerated according to the user request.

## Privacy

- Children and bystanders are not left as recognizable real faces.
- Incidental third parties are stylized, obscured, or left untouched in a privacy-preserving way.
- The final file does not expose unwanted private details introduced during generation.

## Delivery

- Deliver the final image or video file path.
- Mention only important caveats or failed constraints.
- Avoid overexplaining the internal pipeline unless the user asks.
